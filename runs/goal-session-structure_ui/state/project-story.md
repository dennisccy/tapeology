# Project story so far

Tapeology is a trading-research tool that reads live market tape, measures trading ideas honestly, and has now opened up its research work, piece by piece, to a browser view anyone can see.

## How it has grown

Tapeology began as a tool that reads live stock-trade data, judges who's winning it, and pairs that with a journal, replay studies, and an honest profit scorecard; a later addition found real price levels and zones for a second trading approach, though an honest test found no proof yet it beats the original — none of it was visible in the app itself.

A new "Structure" tab then opened that research up for the first time: it first drew levels and zones on a chart, fixing one early case where the chart could go silently blank instead of saying so honestly, then added a Strategy Registry showing both trading approaches side by side with a "Champion" badge confirming the original still holds the title.

The last piece promised all along — a head-to-head comparison screen letting someone pick a dataset, run both strategies with one click, and see trade counts, returns, win rates, and an honest "not enough data yet" label where needed — worked in hands-on testing, but the independent check meant to confirm it couldn't reach the app that round, so the team held it back rather than release it on its own word alone.

This latest, closing round supplied exactly that missing proof. With no new features added, the team first made sure the app started up reliably, then had an independent tester click all the way through the comparison screen from scratch — choosing a dataset, running both strategies, and watching real, matching numbers appear side by side, including the honest "not enough trades yet" result for the newer approach on the data at hand. Every number matched what the app itself actually produced, nothing was faked, and the earlier chart and champion-badge checks held up too. With that evidence in hand, this chapter of work — giving the deeper research a browser home anyone can see and trust — is now complete.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch live trade-by-trade tape reading, keep a trading journal, run replay studies, and check an honest profit scorecard. On its Structure tab, picking a stock and time draws key price levels and graded zones on a chart, shows both trading approaches side by side with the current champion badge, and runs a live head-to-head comparison between the two — with an honest label whenever there isn't enough data to judge fairly, and a standing reminder that every dollar figure shown is simulated, not real.
