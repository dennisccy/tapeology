# Project story so far

Tapeology is a trading-research tool that reads live market tape, measures trading ideas honestly, and is now opening up its research work, piece by piece, to a browser view anyone can see.

## How it has grown

Tapeology began as a tool that reads live stock-trade data and judges whether buyers or sellers are winning the tape, with a journal and replay studies alongside it. It grew into an honest profit-measuring machine with a Performance scorecard, then learned to find real price levels in stored history and group them into graded zones for a second, zone-aware trading approach — an honest test found no proof yet that it beat the original, so the original kept its title. None of this deeper research could be seen in the app itself, until now.

This chapter opened by confirming nothing had broken and that the promised new screen didn't exist yet, then delivered a "Structure" tab where picking a stock and a point in time draws its key price levels on a chart, grouped into strength-graded zones. Early testing caught one narrow case where the chart could go blank instead of honestly saying there was nothing to show; it was fixed the same day, with one more round of testing scheduled to confirm it stuck.

That confirmation is now in. The fix was re-tested from scratch and holds. On the same screen, a new Strategy Registry section now shows both trading approaches — the original and the newer, zone-aware one — side by side, with their entry/exit rules and how the newer one scales its risk and reward by level strength, plus a "Champion" badge confirming which approach currently holds the title (still the original) and that this agrees with what the Performance page already showed. Everything that already worked kept working, with no shortcuts taken.

Next up: a side-by-side comparison showing how the newer trading approach would have performed against the original one, directly on screen.

## What it can do today

The product lets users type in a stock ticker to watch live trade-by-trade tape reading, write trading ideas into a journal, run replay studies, and check an honest profit-and-loss scorecard. On its Structure tab, picking a stock and a point in time draws key price levels and strength-graded zones on a chart, and now also shows the lineup of trading approaches side by side with which one currently holds the champion title — the first two pieces of the app's deeper research made visible in the browser, with a head-to-head comparison still to come.
