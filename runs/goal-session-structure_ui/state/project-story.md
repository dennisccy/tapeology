# Project story so far

Tapeology is a trading-research tool that reads live market tape, measures trading ideas honestly, and is now opening up its research work, piece by piece, to a browser view anyone can see.

## How it has grown

Tapeology began as a tool that reads live stock-trade data and tells a trader, moment to moment, whether buyers or sellers are winning the tape, with a journal for trading ideas and replay studies for practicing against past market action. The next chapter turned that trade-reading skill into an honest profit-measuring machine, with a Performance page showing whether a strategy would have made or lost money — always next to a fair comparison, and honest when there wasn't enough evidence to trust a result. The chapter after that taught Tapeology to find genuine support-and-resistance price levels in stored history, group them into graded zones, and build a second, zone-aware trading approach — an honest head-to-head test against the original found no proof yet that the new approach was better, so the original kept its title. All of that, though, only ever lived in the product's internal tools — nobody could see a price level or a zone by looking at the app itself.

This current chapter set out to fix that, starting with an honest stock-take: the team confirmed nothing had broken, and confirmed the promised new screen genuinely didn't exist yet. Then it built the first real piece: a new "Structure" tab, reachable from the main navigation, where picking a stock and a point in time now draws that symbol's key price levels on a chart and shows how those levels cluster into zones graded by strength. Testing was thorough — of fifteen checks, fourteen passed on the first try, and the one that didn't (a chart that could go blank instead of honestly saying there was nothing to show) was caught and fixed. What's left is a small bit of bookkeeping — updating a couple of internal test records to match the fix already made — and nothing that already worked broke in the process.

## What it can do today

The product lets users type in a stock ticker to watch live trade-by-trade tape reading, write trading ideas into a journal, run replay studies, and check an honest profit-and-loss scorecard on a Performance page. It also now has a Structure tab showing a chosen symbol's computed price levels on a chart and how they group into strength-graded zones — the first piece of the app's deeper research made visible, with the trading-approach lineup and comparison still to come.
