# Project story so far

Tapeology is a tape-reading and trade-journaling tool that watches stock trades as they happen, tells you what the market is actually doing right now, keeps an honest restart-proof record of every thesis you have ever declared against that read, and lets you replay your setup grammar over a historical window to measure how it would have played out.

## How it has grown

The product started as a real-time tape cockpit. Type in a ticker, hit Watch, and the screen fills with live data: recent trades colour-coded by side, 14 calculated tape features, a tape-state verdict with a confidence score, and a price chart with true clock-time candles. The engine is built to be honest: high aggression with no price move reads as absorption rather than control; a weak or mixed tape reads as unclear.

Early iterations built the thesis layer — a "Declare a thesis" strip where you choose a setup type, direction, and required invalidation price. All five verdict states render in real browser pixels. The review pillar followed: a persistent Journal with filterable rows, a review-detail page with frozen statements, independently scored outcome and process grades, execution checks, saved mistake tags from the backend taxonomy, and per-horizon excursion outcomes in R. Iteration 17 made the tape-reading engine truly incremental (~18x speedup on dense real-data streams). Iterations 18 and 19 delivered the Studies page: deterministic replay studies against a seeded random-time null baseline, with honesty stamps, sample caveats, and a "Descriptive only — not trading advice" label always in view.

Iterations 20 through 23 completed the full cue layer. The product gained a management stance (Thesis Intact, Weakening, or Invalidated, with live distance-to-invalidation in dollars and R); an eight-item entry checklist with live measured margins that immediately shows "NO FRESH TAPE" if the feed pauses; and setup-forming hints — amber "Setup forming" cards that appear when a recognisable pattern sustains past a short dwell period, show measured plain-language evidence, cite the user's own study results verbatim (or say plainly "no studied baseline"), and let users one-click prefill the declare form without ever creating a thesis automatically. Iteration 24 completed the live feed-basis badge beside the stream-status indicator, showing "Simulated", "IEX (live)", or "SIP (consolidated)" depending on the watch mode, plus an honest disclosure note on live watches.

Iteration 25 added product-wide copy assurance and the optional audio cue: a permanent automated lint scans every research surface to confirm no surface issues trade commands or price forecasts, and an optional sound toggle (off by default, never persisted) plays a brief beep the moment the verdict or management stance changes when a thesis is active. The single remaining item was making the toggle visible even before a thesis was declared.

Iteration 26 closed that last cue-layer gap. The sound toggle was moved out of the thesis-conditional part of the cockpit into an always-rendered position, so it appears immediately on a fresh page load — you can see it is off, and confirm or change it, before you declare anything. The change touched a single frontend file with zero backend impact. All copy discipline and toggle behaviour (off by default, transition-only, served cooldown) is unchanged. This completed every cue-layer journey. The only remaining work before the goal can be declared achieved is a market-hours evidence sweep — converting a set of historical and live-data journeys from "partially tested" to "fully browser-verified" using real credentialed data. ## What it can do today

The product lets users watch any stock ticker (simulated, historical, or live) and see a real-time cockpit labelled with its data-feed basis alongside live tape-state verdicts, confidence, quotes, recent trades, 14 features, and a price chart at true clock time. Users can declare a trading thesis and watch it judged live across all five verdict states, mark actual entry and exit, see the realized move in R, and close a thesis honestly. Users can see an eight-item entry checklist with live measured margins and an immediate freshness warning when the feed goes stale. Users can see management stance while holding a journaled position, and receive setup-forming hints with measured evidence and honest study citations. Every hint is logged in the Journal's Hints view with a feed-basis stamp. Users can browse the Journal for a full trade history with review grades, excursion outcomes, and honest analytics partitioned by data feed and config fingerprint. Users can run replay studies against a seeded random-time null baseline and compare results side by side. The optional sound toggle — off by default — is now visible on the cockpit at all times, even before a thesis is declared.
