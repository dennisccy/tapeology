# Project story so far

Tapeology is a tape-reading tool that watches stock trades as they happen and tells you, in plain language, what the market is actually doing right now — whether buyers are in control, sellers are pressing, or the tape is unclear — and lets you declare, track, mark, and close your own trading thesis against that live read.

## How it has grown

The product started as a real-time tape cockpit. Type in a ticker, hit Watch, and the screen fills with live data: recent trades colour-coded by side, 14 calculated tape features, a tape-state verdict with a confidence score, and a price chart with true clock-time candles. The engine is built to be honest: high aggression with no price move reads as absorption rather than control; a weak or mixed tape reads as unclear. Symbol search, historical replay, live streaming, and pause/resume all work without tearing down the cockpit.

Early iterations built the thesis layer — a "Declare a thesis" strip on the cockpit where you choose a setup type, direction, and required invalidation price. The verdict engine evaluates your thesis continuously: an absorption-reversal only confirms when price actually flips; a level-break stays pending until price crosses the declared level; a trend-continuation rejects while the opposite side has control; a failed-move fade confirms while the failed push is being absorbed. A bad input is refused immediately with a plain-language message.

After resolving several tooling issues (a missing database migration, a build-tool conflict, and a stale QA server that produced misleading captures), the product reached a reliable verification baseline. By iteration 5, the verdict strip rendered live in a real browser for the first time with moment-correct pixel evidence. Iteration 6 delivered the remaining four verdict-state captures and fixed two correctness defects. Iteration 7 completed the deferred re-captures against a canary-verified fresh server and added user-facing thesis resolution — "Played out" and "Abandon" buttons now save the outcome with timestamps and free the strip for the next thesis. The same fresh pixels revealed a new honesty gap: the "making progress" statement read violated even on a clean confirming tape, because the evaluation code checked adverse activity before weighing dominance.

Iteration 8 fixed that gap and added the journaling half of a trade. The dominance rule in the evaluation engine was rewritten to compare favorable-vs-adverse price impact by plain magnitude — neither side wins unless it is material, and when both are material the larger one rules. The fix was proven in both directions with evaluator-opened pixels: a buyer-dominant confirming tape now reads the statement as met, and a seller-dominant rejecting tape still reads it as violated. On top of that, Mark entry and Mark exit controls now appear on the active thesis strip. The entry price is prefilled from the current last trade, you can edit it, and whatever you type is saved verbatim — no fills, no simulated execution. After both marks are recorded, the strip shows the realized move in R units labeled as a journaled measurement with the spread-at-mark shown alongside. The moment an entry exists, the Abandon button disappears so an open position cannot be silently discarded.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch any stock ticker (simulated, historical, or live) and see a real-time cockpit that identifies buyer control, seller control, bid absorption, ask absorption, and unclear tape with confidence scores. Users can replay historical sessions, stream live tickers, search for symbols, pause and resume a watch without losing state, and view a price chart with tape-state markers on true clock-time candles. Users can declare a trading thesis — choosing a setup type, direction, and invalidation price — and watch the tape judged live with a colour-coded verdict badge and plain-language evidence across all five verdict states. Users can mark their actual entry and exit prices verbatim, see the realized move in R units labeled as a journaled measurement, and close a thesis as played out or abandoned with timestamps recorded. Once an entry mark exists, the Abandon button is hidden so an open position cannot be silently discarded. System-owned outcomes (invalidated, expired) remain protected from user override.
