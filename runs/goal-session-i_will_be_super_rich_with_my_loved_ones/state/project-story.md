# Project story so far

Tapeology is a tape-reading and trade-journaling tool that watches stock trades as they happen, tells you what the market is actually doing right now, keeps an honest restart-proof record of every thesis you have ever declared against that read, and lets you replay your setup grammar over a historical window to measure how it would have played out.

## How it has grown

The product started as a real-time tape cockpit. Type in a ticker, hit Watch, and the screen fills with live data: recent trades colour-coded by side, 14 calculated tape features, a tape-state verdict with a confidence score, and a price chart with true clock-time candles. The engine is built to be honest: high aggression with no price move reads as absorption rather than control; a weak or mixed tape reads as unclear.

Early iterations built the thesis layer — a "Declare a thesis" strip where you choose a setup type, direction, and required invalidation price. All five verdict states render in real browser pixels. The review pillar followed: a persistent Journal with filterable rows, a review-detail page with frozen statements, independently scored outcome and process grades, execution checks, saved mistake tags from the backend taxonomy, and per-horizon excursion outcomes in R. Iteration 17 made the tape-reading engine truly incremental (~18x speedup on dense real-data streams). Iterations 18 and 19 delivered the Studies page: deterministic replay studies against a seeded random-time null baseline, with honesty stamps, sample caveats, and a "Descriptive only — not trading advice" label always in view.

Iterations 20 through 23 completed the full cue layer. The product gained a management stance (Thesis Intact, Weakening, or Invalidated, with live distance-to-invalidation in dollars and R); an eight-item entry checklist with live measured margins that immediately shows "NO FRESH TAPE" if the feed pauses; and setup-forming hints with measured plain-language evidence and honest study citations. Iteration 24 completed the live feed-basis badge (Simulated, IEX, or SIP). Iteration 25 added product-wide copy assurance and an optional sound toggle. Iteration 26 moved the sound toggle into an always-rendered position so it is visible even before a thesis is declared — completing every cue-layer journey.

Iteration 27 was a real-data verification sweep with the live Alpaca credentials now in place. The product pulled 24,619 real Apple trades from a past trading window and correctly resolved nearly all of them as buys or sells (unknown fraction ≈0.004%). Seven journeys that had been "partially tested" were promoted to fully proven with genuine browser and credentialed evidence: replaying a real historical session, three distinct honest edge-case panels (market closed showing the next open, fake symbol rejected, empty window explained), buy/sell side resolution on real data, a true-clock chart axis, the local-timezone window picker, bounded timeout errors, and the stream-closed state. Two legs remained partial (a transient failure panel that the browser could not hold in a single screenshot, and a re-watch that took ~35s rather than the target <3s). The two live-market legs — the IEX badge over a real feed and the stale-then-recover stream gap — are scheduled for the Monday market open.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch any stock ticker (simulated, historical, or live) and see a real-time cockpit labelled with its data-feed basis alongside live tape-state verdicts, confidence, quotes, recent trades with resolved buy/sell sides, 14 features, and a price chart at true clock time. Users can replay historical sessions against real SIP data, pick a window in their local timezone, and change replay speed without restarting. The product shows honest panels when the market is closed, when a symbol is not tradable, or when a window has no data. Users can declare a trading thesis and watch it judged live across all five verdict states, mark actual entry and exit, see the realized move in R, and close a thesis honestly. An eight-item entry checklist with live measured margins gives an immediate freshness warning when the feed goes stale. Management stance and setup-forming hints (with measured evidence and honest study citations) complete the cue layer. Every hint is logged in the Journal's Hints view. Users can browse the Journal for a full trade history with review grades, excursion outcomes, and analytics partitioned by data feed. Users can run replay studies against a seeded random-time null baseline. The optional sound toggle is always visible on the cockpit, off by default.
