# Project story so far

Tapeology is a tape-reading and trade-journaling tool that watches stock trades as they happen, tells you what the market is actually doing right now, and keeps an honest, restart-proof record of every thesis you have ever declared against that read.

## How it has grown

The product started as a real-time tape cockpit. Type in a ticker, hit Watch, and the screen fills with live data: recent trades colour-coded by side, 14 calculated tape features, a tape-state verdict with a confidence score, and a price chart with true clock-time candles. The engine is built to be honest: high aggression with no price move reads as absorption rather than control; a weak or mixed tape reads as unclear. Symbol search, historical replay, live streaming, and pause/resume all work without tearing down the cockpit.

Early iterations built the thesis layer — a "Declare a thesis" strip on the cockpit where you choose a setup type, direction, and required invalidation price. The verdict engine reached a reliable baseline: all five verdict states — confirming, rejecting, weakening, invalidated, and pending — render in real browser pixels. Iterations 6 and 7 completed thesis resolution and the remaining verdict scenarios. Iteration 8 added entry and exit mark recording with spread-at-mark and realized R. Iteration 9 made an entry-marked thesis survive a watch interruption. Iteration 10 brought thesis geometry onto the price chart. Iteration 11 added honest entry-risk advisory chips at declaration.

Iteration 12 turned Tapeology into a two-page product: a persistent navigation bar and a filterable Journal table of every thesis ever declared, confirmed honest across a real backend restart. Iteration 13 completed the journal detail half — click any journal row to open a full review page showing frozen expected-behaviour statements, the complete verdict timeline at true clock time, entry/exit marks, risk flags, and four machine-derived execution checks computed once at resolution. Iteration 14 completed the review pillar: each frozen statement now shows its final status badge, two grade labels appear (outcome and process, independently scored), and the Save Review button is live — pick mistake tags from the backend taxonomy, add a note, and the thesis is permanently marked as reviewed.

Iteration 15 opened the evidence layer. On any thesis that has run its course, the journal detail page now shows a "How far the tape went (R)" section with two clearly separated blocks — one anchored at the moment the tape first confirmed the idea, one anchored at the moment of actual entry. Each block lists every configured time horizon with the maximum favorable move, maximum adverse move, the outcome (did price reach +1R first, −1R first, or neither?), and the spread cost at that anchor. Where the stream ended before a horizon could complete, the outcome reads TRUNCATED rather than being hidden or fabricated. All figures are in R units only, with an explicit "never currency" caption. Theses that predate this feature show an honest "not measured" note. The grade chip colour was also unified across the journal list and detail pages as part of this iteration.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch any stock ticker (simulated, historical, or live) and see a real-time cockpit identifying buyer control, seller control, bid absorption, ask absorption, and unclear tape with confidence scores. Users can replay historical sessions, stream live tickers, search for symbols, pause and resume a watch without losing state, and view a price chart with tape-state markers on true clock-time candles. Users can declare a trading thesis, watch it judged live across all five verdict states with plain-language evidence, see the declared thesis geometry drawn on the chart, mark their actual entry and exit prices verbatim, see the realized move in R units, close a thesis as played out or abandoned, survive a watch interruption with the thesis intact, and receive honest amber entry-risk chips at declaration. Users can navigate to a persistent Journal page, click any row to open a full detail page with frozen statements and final-status badges, outcome and process grades with evidence, execution checks, mistake-tag picker, and a Save Review button. On any ended thesis, users can also read per-horizon excursion outcomes in R units, separately anchored at first confirmation and at actual entry, with spread costs and truncation declared honestly.
