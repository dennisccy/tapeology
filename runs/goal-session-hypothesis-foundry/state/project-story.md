# Project story so far

Tapeology's newest research chapter, the Hypothesis Foundry, aims to turn every already-approved trading idea sitting in the research notes into one fair, no-cheating test — automatically, without a person picking winners along the way.

## How it has grown

The chapter opened by taking stock: the previous chapter was safely archived, its self-extending loop switched off, and a dated handover recorded, though a setup snag briefly blocked proof screenshots.

The second round fixed that snag, gave the Desk page a "Hypothesis Foundry" section naming the active chapter, and built the rulebook for turning an approved idea into a fair, testable specification.

The third round showed the chapter's real starting numbers on screen for the first time and built the reading, list-locking, and record-keeping machinery behind the scenes — all proven correct in private tests, but not yet visible on screen.

The fourth round put that machinery on screen: four practice panels appeared, each clearly marked "practice data, not the real thing," proving the idea-compiling, idea-reading, list-locking, and full-run-checking pieces all give correct answers — including correctly reversing an answer when a bet direction flips. A small, contained issue was also found: a scoring shortcut briefly borrowed and returned inside the running system, logged for cleanup.

The fifth round delivered the chapter's centerpiece: the one real, permanently-recorded decision. All 11 real research ideas on file were checked, and — honestly — none of them was ready to become a real test yet; each was held back for a specific, disclosed reason (an unresolved detail, a past failed attempt, or a stand-in only). An independent reviewer double-checked the result before it was locked in and caught two small mistakes, both fixed first. A new "Epoch / Manifest" panel now shows this real result on the Desk page, marked with a distinct green banner so it can never be confused with the practice panels. Two of those practice panels also got fixed to show details that were missing before. One small, disclosed wrinkle remains open: an early draft of the real result was created and corrected before anything was locked in, and the project owner needs to confirm that was handled correctly before the next step — actually running any surviving ideas through a real test — can begin.

## What it can do today

The product lets an operator open the Desk page and see, in the Hypothesis Foundry section, that this research chapter has begun with the old one safely closed, four practice demonstrations proving the underlying machinery works, and now the one real result: all 11 real research ideas and exactly what happened to each one. No idea survived to become a real test this round, which is an accepted and correctly-labeled outcome, not a failure. The rest of Tapeology (Desk, Cockpit, Structure Map) is untouched and works as before.
