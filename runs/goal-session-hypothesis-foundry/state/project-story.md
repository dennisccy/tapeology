# Project story so far

Tapeology's newest research chapter, the Hypothesis Foundry, aims to turn every already-approved trading idea into one fair, no-cheating test — automatically, without a person picking winners along the way.

## How it has grown

The chapter opened by closing the previous one and switching off its self-extending loop (round 1), added a Hypothesis Foundry section to the Desk page plus the rulebook for turning an idea into a fair test (round 2), showed the chapter's real starting numbers on screen (round 3), and proved the underlying test machinery correct across practice panels, including reversing an answer when a bet direction flips (round 4). Round 5 delivered the centerpiece: the one real, permanently-recorded decision — all 11 real research ideas checked, none ready yet, each held back for a disclosed reason.

Round 6 ran the one real evaluation pass over that frozen, empty plan, proving with a real timestamp and an independently-checked data fingerprint that the run happened and touched no protected data — an honest "nothing to evaluate" outcome. Checking this closely caught two problems: a summary count worked out two conflicting ways in two files, one just permanently locked, and opening the page quietly writing a small bookkeeping file behind the scenes.

Round 7 fixed the conflicting-count problem the only legal way available, moving the calculation to the one place still allowed to change and adding a permanent agreement check — leaving the bookkeeping-file question and one earlier miscount for the project owner to rule on.

Round 8 finished the chapter's last planned piece: a Final Summary screen tying every panel together, showing how each of the 11 ideas was ruled on, that zero results survived, and letting an operator click into any idea for its full written reasoning. Everything now worked, but two small honesty questions still needed the owner's answer, so the round paused rather than closing.

Round 9, the closing round, changed nothing about the product. The owner had by then written down answers to both open questions, so the team re-ran every part of the chapter from scratch — all eight pieces, the full test suite, the file-integrity locks — and everything still held. The chapter is now finished: every part works, nothing broke, and the two open questions are recorded as accepted, known, non-urgent items, not hidden away. Two small loose ends are noted for later, unrelated work — an old test that may need a fix eventually, and a broken demo-recording script — neither affecting what the chapter does.

## What it can do today

The product lets an operator open the Desk page and see the whole Hypothesis Foundry chapter in one place: it started fresh with the old auto-continuing process turned off, the fair-test rules keep each idea's original timing and direction, the one real result (11 ideas checked, none ready yet — an accepted, honest outcome) is on screen, the one real evaluation pass over that result is confirmed complete, and a Final Summary screen ties it all together with zero survivors this round plus the full written reasoning behind every idea one click away. The rest of Tapeology (Desk, Cockpit, Structure Map) is untouched and works as before.
