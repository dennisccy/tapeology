# Project story so far

Tapeology's newest research chapter, the Hypothesis Foundry, aims to turn every already-approved trading idea sitting in the research notes into one fair, no-cheating test — automatically, without a person picking winners along the way.

## How it has grown

The chapter opened by taking stock: the previous chapter was confirmed safely archived, its self-extending loop switched off, and a dated handover recorded — though a setup snag briefly blocked proof screenshots from being taken.

The second round fixed that snag honestly, gave the Desk page a new "Hypothesis Foundry" section naming the active chapter, and built the rulebook for turning an approved idea into a fair, testable specification, proven correct on seven practice examples.

The third round made the Foundry section show its real starting numbers for the first time, and built five more behind-the-scenes pieces: a reader that reaches the same yes/no decision as Tapeology's older, trusted method, a piece that limits how many versions of one idea are allowed, a piece that locks the whole idea list before any result is seen, and a permanent record book for every trial — all proven correct in private tests, none yet visible on screen.

The fourth round built that screen. The Desk page's Hypothesis Foundry section grew four new panels, each clearly marked "practice data, not the real thing": one shows research ideas turning into fair test specs, one shows the idea-reading machinery reaching the exact same calls as the old trusted method — including correctly reversing its answer when a bet is flipped from betting a price rises to betting it falls — one shows the variant-limiting and list-locking machinery holding firm through a simulated crash, and one shows a full practice run covering every kind of outcome landing correctly. Two of the four panels are now complete; the other two are still missing a couple of details their own checklists promised. A small, contained issue was also found and logged — a scoring shortcut briefly borrowed and returned inside the running system — which must be cleaned up before this chapter can close.

## What it can do today

The product lets an operator open the Desk page and see, in the Hypothesis Foundry section, that a new self-contained research chapter has begun with the old one safely closed off, the real starting numbers this chapter will be measured against, and four practice panels proving the idea-compiling, idea-reading, list-locking, and full-run-checking machinery all give correct answers on built-in test cases. No real research idea has been fed through it yet — that is the next milestone. The rest of Tapeology (the Desk, Cockpit, and Structure Map) is untouched and works exactly as before.
