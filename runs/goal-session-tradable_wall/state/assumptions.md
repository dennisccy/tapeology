**Reversible:** yes

## iter-5 — goal-decomposer

**Ambiguity:** The goal (J-02/J-05 acceptance, Data Contract) is silent on how a touch event in the most-recent stored session should be presented when its reaction is computed from a truncated sub-horizon (`setups.py` caps the reaction read at `min(touch_index + horizons[0], len(all_bars)-1)`) while its horizon-0 forward return honestly reports `None` — the audit-B1 case, 13/801 live events. Should such a boundary event surface its (shorter) effective horizon, have its reaction flag-suppressed, or be excluded entirely from the registry/edge-report?
**We chose:** Additive disclosure — the event KEEPS its existing `reaction` label and `forward_returns`, and additively carries the effective reaction horizon + a boundary flag so the iter-6 UI can render it honestly as truncated-horizon. No label mutation, no exclusion. This preserves byte-identity of every non-boundary event (J-02 pinned AAPL 06-22 unchanged; J-04 edge-report cells unchanged) and keeps the gate-honesty rails intact, while resolving the "definitive label beside None returns" inconsistency by disclosing it rather than hiding it. A human who prefers suppressing the boundary reaction or excluding boundary events from the registry could reverse this.
**Reversible:** yes
