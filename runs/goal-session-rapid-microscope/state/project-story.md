# Project story so far

Tapeology is a market-research tool that studies real price and order-flow data honestly, without ever placing a real trade or giving trading advice.

## How it has grown

Earlier chapters built a chart-watching Cockpit, a wall-map Structure page, a pattern-checking Desk, and a Referee, then added a faster way to test small ideas: a data-readiness panel, a tick-by-tick pressure reader, a signal matcher, a permanent idea ledger, a walk-forward checker, a sealing Vault, and a Graduation check. Later rounds hardened the Vault's hardest failure modes, put the research work on screen as three Desk panels — a Scout Ledger, a Walk-Forward panel, and a read-only Vault view — and opened it all to a Claude conversation through four read-only channels.

A run of rounds then tightened the safety checks guarding the Graduation test, the one check that decides whether a trading idea is ever allowed to "pass." These rounds fixed a blind safety test, closed the project's oldest open question about a mistimed liquidity reading, made the Graduation judge own its own minimum sample size, and proved that re-running the same research work over the same stored data always gives back the same numbers.

Three rounds ago, the project opened its last major piece: three pre-declared "pilot studies," small honest tests of specific trading ideas anchored to the exact moment a real trade touches a support/resistance wall. The first study — does price making a new high while buying pressure weakens predict a reversal — was run and honestly came back "not enough data yet." This round finished the job: the two remaining studies — does aggressive buying or selling into a price wall get rejected, and does a capitulation moment predict exhaustion — were wired up the same way and also came back with honest "not enough data yet" answers, each now a permanent record an operator can trigger from the command line or the product's own trigger. The Graduation check was also re-confirmed with a fresh look. That makes nine of the ten planned capabilities proven and green.

The one piece still not done is recording a batch of real market data into the sealed Vault — an act only the project's owner can authorise, attend, and approve, because it can never be undone. The project cannot finish on its own until the owner decides: record the real data, change what that last piece is asked to do, or accept the project as done without it.

## What it can do today

The product lets people see, on the Desk page, how much market data is on hand and which research checks remain unmet. It tracks buying and selling pressure tick by tick, matched to chart signals without looking ahead, and keeps a permanent, unhideable record of every quick trading idea it tests — now including all three pre-declared pilot studies, each with an honest recorded answer. A panel shows how those ideas hold up over time, and a freshly-proven check tells whether any idea has "graduated" to a fuller test. A read-only panel shows sealed recordings without revealing their contents, and a Claude conversation can read all of this the same way a person would on screen.
