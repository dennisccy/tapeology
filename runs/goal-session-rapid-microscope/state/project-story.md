# Project story so far

Tapeology is a market-research tool that studies real price and order-flow data honestly, without ever placing a real trade or giving trading advice.

## How it has grown

The product has grown through several chapters already: a chart-watching Cockpit, a Structure page that maps price walls and turning points, a Desk page where a playbook of chart patterns gets checked against that wall map, and — most recently — a built-in Referee that judges whether any idea actually holds up, using statistics designed to disprove its own findings rather than flatter them.

This new chapter, "The Rapid Microscope," opens right after the Referee was finished. The Referee is deliberately slow and careful, which is right for a final verdict but means new ideas wait a long time before getting even a first look. This chapter's goal is to build the front of that pipeline: a fast, honest way to try many small ideas about what happens inside a price move, and quickly rule out the ones that don't hold up, so only the strongest ideas ever reach the Referee.

The opening step did no new building, on purpose. The team spent it checking that everything already shipped — the Cockpit, the Structure map, and the Desk screen — still works exactly as before, and wrote down the starting numbers this chapter will be measured against: how many of the product's built-in tests pass, and two technical checks that prove nothing was quietly changed. Every one of the ten things this chapter promises to eventually deliver was confirmed not yet built — an honest, by-design starting point, not a setback.

Nothing in this chapter works for users yet. The next step is a new "Microscope Readiness" section on the Desk page that honestly reports how much market data is actually on hand to study.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch live and historical price charts, see mapped-out price walls and turning points, review a playbook of chart-pattern signals checked against those walls, and browse the Referee's own record of ideas it has judged. This new chapter has not added anything new for users yet.
