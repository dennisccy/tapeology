# Project story so far

Tapeology is a research app with three screens — a live-tape Cockpit, a Structure page that maps where a stock tends to bounce or stall, and a Desk that scans for chart setups and logs every signal it sees, honestly, without ever placing a real trade.

## How it has grown

This chapter, "The Referee," taught the product to check its own homework — telling a real pattern apart from one imagined in noise — with a shared evidence record, a statistics engine, matched "nothing happened" comparisons, and a permanent notebook that locks in a question's start date.

The Desk page then gained a "Referee Registry" screen for reviewing and registering research questions, and a hard rule that the core trading strategy can never be swapped without a genuine matching certificate — a real gap in that rule was found and closed by the team itself.

It then gained its last two Referee sections — "Referee Adjudications" for each question's plain verdict, and "Referee Runs" for starting and watching a check — and the Claude assistant connector grew from 20 to 22 tools; a verification-only round then closed the chapter clean the first time, with all ten planned abilities standing on freshly-checked evidence.

One more question was added afterward: the wait the Referee Registry quotes before a question has enough evidence was measured only in raw calendar days, which a long silent recording gap could inflate. This round fixed that — the Referee Registry now also states, in plain words, how many sessions are truly on record, the span of dates they cover, and how long the longest silent stretch was, plus a new column showing the wait measured in recorded sessions, right beside the unchanged old calendar-day number. The team checked every figure by hand against the real numbers on screen. All eleven planned abilities now stand on freshly-checked evidence, and the chapter closes clean a second time.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch the live tape on the Cockpit, check a stock's price map on the Structure page, and scan chart setups on the Desk. On the Desk page, users can open "Referee Registry" to review and register a research question — now with an honest note on how many sessions are truly on record and the wait measured in sessions, not just calendar days — "Referee Adjudications" for each question's plain verdict, and "Referee Runs" to start and watch a check. The core trading strategy stays protected: it cannot be replaced without a genuine, matching certificate.
