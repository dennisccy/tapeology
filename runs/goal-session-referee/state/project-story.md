# Project story so far

Tapeology is a research app with three screens — a live-tape Cockpit, a Structure page that maps where a stock tends to bounce or stall, and a Desk that scans for chart setups and logs every signal it sees, honestly, without ever placing a real trade.

## How it has grown

This chapter, "The Referee," taught the product to check its own homework — telling a real pattern apart from imagined noise — with a shared evidence record, a statistics engine, matched "nothing happened" comparisons, and a notebook that locks in a question's start date. It reached the Desk page as "Referee Registry" (register and review a question), "Referee Adjudications" (each question's plain verdict), and "Referee Runs" (start and watch a check); the Claude assistant connector grew from 20 to 22 tools, and a hard rule now stops the core trading strategy from ever being swapped without genuine matching proof. A verification-only round then closed this first stage clean, with ten abilities standing on freshly-checked evidence.

Next, the Referee Registry's wait-estimate — previously stated only in raw calendar days, which a long silent recording gap could inflate — gained an honest note: how many sessions are truly on record, the date span they cover, and the longest silent stretch, plus a new column showing the wait measured in recorded sessions beside the unchanged calendar-day figure. All eleven abilities then stood on freshly-checked evidence, and the chapter closed clean a second time.

A twelfth question then arrived: the evidence behind each research family — chart-pattern and strategy-trade counts, and why the strategy side isn't ready to be judged yet — had always been tracked, but was only visible to a technical lookup, never on screen. This round put it on screen inside "Referee Registry," as a "Playbook Family" block and a "Strategy Family" block, including the strategy block's honest warning that it doesn't yet have enough evidence, plus a caveat about not peeking at data from before it existed. The team checked it on two separate test setups — one with real evidence, one empty — and both showed honest numbers, never a blank screen. The chapter isn't declared finished this round, though: two older pieces were skipped for lack of time, and one screenshot of the new warning message was cut off partway, though the team confirmed by other means that the message itself is genuinely there and correct.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch the live tape on the Cockpit, check a stock's price map on the Structure page, and scan chart setups on the Desk. On the Desk page's "Referee Registry," users can register a research question, see a plain verdict for each one, start and watch a check, see the wait in both calendar days and true recorded sessions, and now see exactly how much evidence backs each question and why the strategy side isn't ready yet. The core trading strategy stays protected: it cannot be replaced without genuine, matching proof.
