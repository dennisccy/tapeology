# Project story so far

Tapeology is a research app with three screens — a live-tape Cockpit, a Structure page that maps where a stock tends to bounce or stall, and a Desk that scans for chart setups and logs every signal it sees, honestly, without ever placing a real trade.

## How it has grown

This chapter, "The Referee," taught the product to check its own homework — telling a real pattern apart from imagined noise. It built a shared evidence record, a statistics engine, matched "nothing happened" comparisons, and a notebook that locks in a question's start date, then put all of it on the Desk page as "Referee Registry," "Referee Adjudications," and "Referee Runs." The Claude assistant connector grew from 20 to 22 tools, and a hard rule now stops the core trading strategy from ever being swapped without genuine matching proof. A verification round closed that first stage clean, and a later round made the wait-estimate honest too — showing how many trading sessions are truly on record and the longest silent stretch, not just raw calendar days — closing clean a second time.

A twelfth question then arrived: the evidence behind each research family had always been tracked but never shown on screen. A round added two new blocks to "Referee Registry" — "Playbook Family" and "Strategy Family," the latter carrying an honest warning that it doesn't yet have enough evidence, plus a caveat about not peeking at data from before it existed. That round could not close the chapter: two older checks had been skipped for time, and the new warning's photo was cut off partway down a very long page.

The most recent round finished both loose ends without changing the product at all. It ran the two skipped checks for real — one proving the historical record still reconciles correctly, the other proving a question's evidence still keeps the right shape — and re-took the cut-off photo, capturing just that one message this time. With every promise now backed by fresh, independently-checked proof, the team closed "The Referee" chapter, and a second, independent review agreed.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch the live tape on the Cockpit, check a stock's price map on the Structure page, and scan chart setups on the Desk. On the Desk page's "Referee Registry," users can register a research question, see a plain verdict for each one, start and watch a check, see the wait in both calendar days and true recorded sessions, and see exactly how much evidence backs each question — including an honest warning when the strategy side isn't ready to be judged yet. The core trading strategy stays protected: it cannot be replaced without genuine, matching proof.
