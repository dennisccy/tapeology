# Project story so far

Tapeology is a research app with three screens: a Cockpit that reads the live tape as it happens, a Structure page that maps where a stock tends to bounce or stall, and a Desk that scans for chart setups and writes down every signal it sees, honestly, without ever placing a real trade. This new chapter, "The Referee," is teaching the desk something it has never had before: a way to check its own homework.

## How it has grown

Five earlier chapters built the whole working product you see today: the live Cockpit that reads market pressure as it happens, the Structure page that maps the price levels where a stock tends to bounce or stall, and the Desk page that scans for chart setups, grades them, and keeps an honest paper record of every signal it finds. By the end of that work, the desk could already spot patterns worth a second look — trades bouncing off a price level, or a market snapping back hard after a sharp sell-off.

But a pattern someone notices by eye is not proof the pattern is real. Until now, nothing in Tapeology could tell the difference between a real edge and a shape a person imagined in the noise. This new chapter starts building that check.

The first round of this chapter did no building at all, on purpose. The team spent it confirming, address by address and screen by screen, that none of the new fact-checking tools exist yet — and, just as importantly, that every part of the existing product still works exactly as before. They watched a live simulated stock update on the Cockpit, loaded Apple's real price levels on the Structure page, and opened every section of the Desk page, and all of it worked. Nothing was broken, and nothing new has shipped yet. The next round starts the real building: a private count of how much trading evidence the desk has already recorded — the first piece the rest of this chapter depends on.

## What it can do today

The product itself hasn't gained anything new in this chapter yet. The team spent the first round confirming that everything built in earlier chapters — the live Cockpit, the Structure price-map, and every Desk section — still works exactly as it did before. None of this chapter's fact-checking tools exist in the product yet; that work starts next round.
