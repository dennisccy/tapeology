# Project story so far

Tapeology is a research app with three screens — a live-tape Cockpit, a Structure page that maps where a stock tends to bounce or stall, and a Desk that scans for chart setups and logs every signal it sees, honestly, without ever placing a real trade.

## How it has grown

This chapter, "The Referee," taught the product to check its own homework — telling a real pattern apart from one imagined in noise. It first built the checking machinery behind the scenes: a shared evidence record, a statistics engine, matched "nothing happened" comparisons, and a permanent notebook that locks in a question's start date and turns it into one verdict no later check can change.

The Desk page then got its first Referee screen, "Referee Registry," for reviewing and registering research questions, plus a hard rule: the core trading strategy can never be swapped for a new one without a genuine certificate from the fact-checking system. The team later found and closed a real gap in that rule on its own — a certificate could once be stamped with a strategy's name without its evidence truly belonging to that strategy.

The Desk page then gained its last two Referee sections — "Referee Adjudications," showing every question's plain verdict and evidence trail, and "Referee Runs," for starting a check and watching it run, cancel, and finish — and the Claude assistant connector grew from 20 to 22 tools.

This final round did no building at all — it only proved what was already there. It re-ran seven parts of the system a rushed earlier round had left half-checked, and took the one photograph still owed: proof the Desk page really does refuse a second background check while one is already running. Every one of the chapter's ten promised abilities now stands on real, freshly-checked evidence, nothing was found broken, and the chapter closes clean — zero real research questions written down yet and zero "corroborated" verdicts, which for a system built to be honest about doubt is exactly what finishing well looks like.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch the live tape on the Cockpit, check a stock's price map on the Structure page, and scan chart setups on the Desk. On the Desk page, users can open "Referee Registry" to review and register a research question, "Referee Adjudications" to see each question's plain verdict and evidence trail, and "Referee Runs" to start a check and watch its progress and history. The core trading strategy stays protected — it cannot be replaced unless a genuine, matched certificate proves the replacement earned it.
