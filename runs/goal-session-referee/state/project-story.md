# Project story so far

Tapeology is a research app with three screens — a live-tape Cockpit, a Structure page that maps where a stock tends to bounce or stall, and a Desk that scans for chart setups and logs every signal it sees, honestly, without ever placing a real trade.

## How it has grown

This chapter, "The Referee," is teaching the product to check its own homework — telling a pattern noticed by eye apart from a shape imagined in noise. It opened by confirming everything old still worked, then built the checking machinery behind the scenes: a shared evidence record, a statistics engine for judging whether a pattern is real (tightened once after being caught overconfident), a way to compare every signal against fair "nothing happened" moments, and a permanent notebook where a question is locked in — start date included — the moment it's written, closing a backdating hole before anything real was saved.

Then came the actual judge: a written-down question can be measured against its fair comparisons and return one permanent verdict no later check can change. Testing it turned up two soft spots — a failed self-check could still let a false answer through, and a damaged record could vanish instead of warning anyone — both now closed.

This round gave a person something to actually click. The Desk page now has a "Referee Registry" section listing the five candidate research questions the team pre-approved, each with a plain-English reason and live evidence counts. Pick one, confirm, and it registers for real — permanently, today's date locked in as the starting line. A registered question shows two counts side by side: evidence from before registration ("discovery" — interesting, never proof) and evidence collected since (what will decide the answer). Checking the new screen, the team caught a real number that was wrong — a "days until ready" estimate that counted old evidence as progress and claimed some questions were ready right now, when the honest wait is 50 to 119 days — and fixed it the same round, before anyone acted on it.

Next up: a rule that stops any new trading strategy from being approved unless it has passed a real check from this fact-checking system, with no way around it.

## What it can do today

The product lets users watch the live tape on the Cockpit, look up a stock's price map on the Structure page, and scan chart setups on the Desk — the same three screens as before. New this round: on the Desk page, users can open "Referee Registry" to see five candidate research questions with live evidence counts and register one for real, permanent tracking.
