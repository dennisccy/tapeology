# Project story so far

Tapeology watches a stock's live trade-by-trade order flow to judge whether buyers or sellers are in control, and it has an honest engine for measuring whether that read turns into real profit — its newest chapter anchored that read to real price structure, support and resistance, and has now completed and honestly measured a second trading approach built on it.

## How it has grown

Earlier chapters built the live tape reader, journal, replay studies, and an honest profit-measuring engine that proved the original trading rule loses money on real data — a frozen foundation nothing since has been allowed to disturb. This chapter then gave Tapeology a multi-year, multi-timeframe price history, genuine support/resistance levels, and graded A/B/C confluence zones, before turning those zones into a second experimental trading rule that fires only when price sits at a zone and the tape agrees, with its own class-scaled sizing, stops, and profit targets.

This round, Tapeology finished the piece the whole chapter was building toward: its honest profit-measuring tool now compares the new zone-aware rule against the original rule head-to-head — on data it trained on and on data it had never seen — promoting the new rule only if it genuinely wins on the unseen data with enough trades to trust, and refusing to count a training-only win as real. Run against the one small sample of history on hand today, the tool is honest about its limits: too few unseen-data trades yet to call a winner either way, so the original rule keeps its title for now. Every independent check — an outside reviewer, a QA pass, and a skeptical audit — confirmed the comparison tool itself works correctly and honestly, and a final independent confirmation has now closed out this research chapter as complete: every planned capability was built, and none of it broke anything that came before.

With this chapter complete, the natural next step is gathering more real market history so the new zone-aware approach gets a fair, larger-scale test — the honest comparison tool is ready and waiting for it.

## What it can do today

The product lets users type a stock ticker and watch live trade-by-trade action to see who's in control, journal trading ideas, run replay studies, and backtest strategies into honest profit-or-loss reports beside a random-guess comparison, viewable on a Performance page — with AI assistants able to read all of it directly. This chapter's price history, support/resistance levels, zone grading, the zone-aware trading rule with its own sizing and stops, and the honest head-to-head comparison against the original rule all now exist, work correctly, and have been proven not to break anything that came before — though they remain reachable only through the product's internal machine connection, not yet through the visible app.
