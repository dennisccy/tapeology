## What you can do today

Tapeology gives you a fast, honest read on what one US stock's order flow is doing right now. With the finished product you can:

- **Watch any one US stock at a time** and get a live, plain-English read of its tape — updating in real time as new trades and quotes arrive.
- **See who's in control:** whether buyers or sellers are driving the tape, whether heavy buying or selling is being quietly *absorbed* while the price holds steady, or whether the action is simply unclear — each with a confidence score.
- **Read the full cockpit** for the stock you're watching: a live bid, ask, spread and last price; a running list of recent trades; the core order-flow readouts; plain-language observations; and an event log that flags the moment the read changes.
- **Count on its honesty.** When one side is trading hard but the price refuses to move, it says "absorption," not "control"; when the evidence is mixed, it says "unclear" rather than faking a confident call; and the numbers you see are worked out once and shown identically everywhere.
- **Choose your data source** — built-in practice data, a real past market session, or a real live feed.
- **Replay a real past session:** pick a real US stock, a past date, a time window, and a replay speed, and watch that session's real trades and quotes play back.
- **Follow a stock live, in real time** during market hours, with a green "live" light — and an honest amber "stale" light (and zero invented trades) whenever the feed briefly goes quiet, returning to live the moment real data resumes.
- **Find a stock** by typing part of its name or ticker and picking from real suggestions.
- **Know the market's hours** — see whether the US market is open or closed, and when it next opens.
- **Stop and restart cleanly** at any time.
- **Never get a made-up screen.** When real data isn't available — no account connected, an unknown stock, an empty window, or a closed market — you get a clear, specific message instead of fabricated prices.

## How it came together

**Practice mode first.** Before touching a single piece of real market data, the product was proven on built-in practice data that let us get the hard ideas right. The single-stock "cockpit" came to life, along with the product's defining habit of honesty: recognizing when heavy buying or selling is being *absorbed* — the price simply refuses to move — instead of mistaking that pressure for genuine control. This session began with a clean check-up confirming the practice cockpit still worked from end to end.

**Choosing your data source.** Next came a simple selector at the top of the screen — Live, Historical, or Simulated — with each choice revealing only the controls it needs. From the very start, picking a real-data mode without a market-data account connected produced a clear "real-data unavailable" message rather than ever inventing a price.

**Replaying real history.** Then real market data began to flow. In Historical mode you can enter a real US stock, pick a past date, a time window, and a replay speed, and watch that session's real trades and quotes play back through the same core that powers practice mode. A search box began suggesting real stocks as you type, and dead ends — an unknown stock, or a window with no data — started telling the truth in plain words instead of faking a screen.

**Knowing the market's hours.** The Live view gained a real market-status indicator — "open," or "closed" with the time it next opens — and trying to watch a stock live while the market is closed now shows an honest "market is closed" message rather than an empty or invented screen.

**Going live.** The final step completed the promise: the product now follows a stock live, in real time. Choose Live, enter a ticker, press Watch, and during market hours the screen streams the market's real trades and quotes with a green "live" light. If the feed goes quiet, an honest amber "stale" light appears and no trades are invented during the lull — then it returns to live the moment real data resumes. With that, every must-have was complete.
