- Pull up a stock's permanently stored price history across multiple time scales, from hourly moves up to monthly trends, each one checked for tampering every time it's read back. - See the genuine support-and-resistance price levels Tapeology has worked out from that history — the price points where a stock has tended to turn, each one scored for how strong it is. - See those levels grouped into zones wherever several time scales agree with each other, each zone honestly graded on an A, B, or C scale for how convincing it is. - Run a brand-new, experimental trading approach that only acts when price is sitting right at one of these graded zones and the live tape agrees at that exact moment — sizing its simulated bets and setting its stop and profit target more tightly at the strongest zones and more cautiously at the weaker ones. - See an honest head-to-head report comparing this new zone-aware approach against the original one — trained on some history and tested on history it has never seen — that only crowns a new winner once it genuinely proves itself with enough evidence, and says so plainly when it doesn't.

None of this new research work is reachable from the app's screens yet — only through the product's internal tools and its AI-assistant connection.

How it came together

This chapter opened with a full health check, confirming that everything built in earlier chapters — the live tape reading, the journal, the replay studies, the honest profit-measuring engine — still worked exactly as it always had, before any new work began.

Next, Tapeology learned to fetch and permanently remember a stock's real historical price movement across multiple time scales — daily, weekly, monthly, and hourly and more — complete with built-in tamper detection and an honest "please connect your data account" message whenever no data connection is configured.

With that history in hand, Tapeology then worked out the genuine support-and-resistance price levels hiding inside it — the price points where a stock has tended to turn — each one scored for strength, and proven never to sneak a look at data from the future.

Those levels were then grouped into zones wherever several time scales agreed with each other, with each zone honestly graded on an A, B, or C scale for how convincing it is.

Tapeology then gained a second, experimental trading approach that only acts when price is sitting right at one of these graded zones and the live tape agrees at that exact moment — either the zone holds and price bounces back, or the zone breaks and price keeps going with real conviction.

That new approach then learned to size its simulated bets and set its stop and profit target based on how strong each zone is — tighter stops and bigger simulated bets at the strongest zones, more caution at the weaker ones — with its results checkable zone-strength by zone-strength instead of as one blended number.

Finally, Tapeology's honest comparison tool learned to test this whole new approach head-to-head against the original one — training on some history and testing on history it had never seen — crowning a new champion only if it genuinely proved better with enough evidence behind it. Run against today's one small sample of real history, it honestly found there wasn't yet enough new evidence either way, so the original approach kept its title. An outside reviewer, a quality check, and a skeptical audit each independently confirmed the comparison tool itself works correctly and honestly, and nothing built along the way ever broke what came before — closing this chapter out complete. What's left is not more building but more data: gathering a larger real market history so this new zone-aware approach gets a fair, larger-scale test.
