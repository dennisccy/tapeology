# Demo Script — goal-desk-iter-10

**Mode:** record
**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Frontend URL:** http://localhost:3301
**Iteration:** 10

## Highlights

### Step 01 — Open the live Cockpit

- **Narration:** Tapeology opens on the Cockpit, where a stock's price action can update live in front of you.
- **Action:** Navigate to /
- **Point out:** The Tapeology home screen, ready to watch a ticker.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-desk-iter-10/step-01.png

### Step 02 — Choose the simulated tape

- **Narration:** You can watch a simulated stock first. You do not need a live market feed to see how a read works.
- **Action:** Click the "Simulated" button
- **Point out:** The Simulated option is selected and ready for a ticker name.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-desk-iter-10/step-02.png

### Step 03 — Name the simulated ticker

- **Narration:** Typing a name sets up which simulated stock the tape will follow.
- **Action:** Type "SIM-BUYER" into the "Ticker" field
- **Point out:** The ticker field now holds the name just typed.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-desk-iter-10/step-03.png

### Step 04 — Watch the tape settle into a read

- **Narration:** Price bars move in real time. The tape settles into a plain-English read, like "Buyer Control."
- **Action:** Click the "Watch" button
- **Point out:** The live read label above the moving price bars.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-desk-iter-10/step-04.png

### Step 05 — Open the Desk

- **Narration:** The Desk lists about 100 stocks, ranked by how close each one sits to a key price level. Each row also says how old its price reading is.
- **Action:** Navigate to /desk
- **Point out:** The ranked rows, and the panel naming exactly which price data built this screen.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-desk-iter-10/step-05.png

### Step 06 — Look back at a past scan

- **Narration:** Every past screen is saved. Look back at any of them, exactly as it was recorded.
- **Action:** Click "[data-testid="desk-history-row"][data-screen-date="2026-06-22"]"
- **Point out:** A note confirming this is an older, saved screen, not today's.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-desk-iter-10/step-06.png

### Step 07 — Jump from a row into its chart

- **Narration:** Clicking any row opens that stock's detailed chart, already loaded for the same date.
- **Action:** Click "desk-screen-row"
- **Point out:** The Structure page opens, pre-filled from the row just clicked.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-desk-iter-10/step-07.png
