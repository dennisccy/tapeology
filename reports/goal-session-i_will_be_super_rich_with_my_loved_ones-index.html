Project Goal

Tapeology is a **standalone, real-time tape-reading system for US stocks**. It does one thing well: given a single ticker, it watches live order flow — trades, quotes, and (later) the Level 2 book — and classifies the **current tape state**.

The story so far

Project story so far

Tapeology is a tape-reading and trade-journaling tool that watches stock trades as they happen, tells you what the market is actually doing right now, keeps an honest restart-proof record of every thesis you have ever declared against that read, and lets you replay your setup grammar over a historical window to measure how it would have played out.

How it has grown

The product started as a real-time tape cockpit. Type in a ticker, hit Watch, and the screen fills with live data: recent trades colour-coded by side, 14 calculated tape features, a tape-state verdict with a confidence score, and a price chart with true clock-time candles. The engine is built to be honest: high aggression with no price move reads as absorption rather than control; a weak or mixed tape reads as unclear.

Early iterations built the thesis layer — a "Declare a thesis" strip where you choose a setup type, direction, and required invalidation price. All five verdict states render in real browser pixels. The review pillar followed: a persistent Journal with filterable rows, a review-detail page with frozen statements, independently scored outcome and process grades, execution checks, saved mistake tags from the backend taxonomy, and per-horizon excursion outcomes in R. Iteration 17 made the tape-reading engine truly incremental (~18x speedup on dense real-data streams). Iterations 18 and 19 delivered the Studies page: deterministic replay studies against a seeded random-time null baseline, with honesty stamps, sample caveats, and a "Descriptive only — not trading advice" label always in view. That closed the "evidence before cues" gate, allowing the cue layer to begin.

Iterations 20 and 21 shipped the first two cues: a management stance (Thesis Intact, Weakening, or Invalidated, with live distance-to-invalidation in dollars and R) while holding a position, and an eight-item entry checklist with live measured margins before marking entry. Iteration 22 closed a freshness defect that had been confirmed live in iteration 21: the checklist was going stale when the feed was paused, keeping a frozen green "conditions met" over a tape that was no longer live. The fix routes every non-terminal status change through a fresh read of the engine's current snapshot, so the degraded "NO FRESH TAPE" stance publishes immediately. A live data-lag readout now appears next to the stream status dot.

Iteration 23 completed the last unbuilt cue surface. When the tape sustains a recognisable absorption or control pattern past a short dwell period, an amber "Setup forming" card appears in the cockpit. It names the pattern, shows measured evidence in plain language (for example, "Bid absorption is sustained 5 seconds — aggressive selling is being absorbed at the bid with no meaningful downward price progress"), and either cites the user's own study results verbatim or says plainly "no studied baseline — unvalidated pattern". A single click prefills the thesis declare form with the matching setup type and direction — but the user must still type the invalidation price, and no thesis is ever created by the click alone. The card disappears the moment the tape changes or the feed pauses, so present-tense "is forming" copy never sits over a dead tape. Every hint the cockpit has ever shown is permanently logged in a new Hints tab in the Journal, with timestamps, pattern, evidence, baseline citation, and whether it was used to prefill a declaration.

What it can do today

The product lets users watch any stock ticker (simulated, historical, or live) and see a real-time cockpit identifying buyer control, seller control, bid and ask absorption, and unclear tape. Users can replay historical sessions, stream live tickers, search for symbols, pause and resume a watch, and view a price chart with tape-state markers at true clock time. Users can declare a trading thesis and watch it judged live across all five verdict states with plain-language evidence, mark their actual entry and exit verbatim, see the realized move in R units, and close a thesis honestly. Users can navigate to a persistent Journal, open any thesis for a full review with frozen statements, final-status badges, outcome and process grades, execution checks, and saved mistake tags. On any ended thesis, users can read per-horizon excursion outcomes in R anchored separately at confirmation and at entry. Users can switch the Journal to an Analytics view for honest, segregated statistics partitioned by data feed and config fingerprint. Users can navigate to the Studies page, run a deterministic replay study of a setup grammar over a chosen window, and compare results side-by-side with a seeded random-time null baseline. Before marking entry on an active thesis, users see an eight-item entry checklist with live measured margins, an overall stance chip, and a nearest-counterevidence line — and if the feed pauses or goes stale, that checklist immediately says so. A live data-lag readout appears beside the stream status indicator. While holding a journaled position, users see at a glance whether the tape still supports their thesis. When a sustained absorption or control pattern forms, the cockpit shows a descriptive "Setup forming" card with measured evidence and an honest study-baseline citation, and lets users one-click prefill a thesis declaration. Every hint is logged in the Journal's Hints view.
