The story so far

Project story so far

Tapeology is a trading-research tool that reads live market tape, measures trading ideas honestly, and is now opening up its research work, piece by piece, to a browser view anyone can see.

How it has grown

Tapeology began as a tool that reads live stock-trade data, judges whether buyers or sellers are winning the tape, and pairs that with a journal, replay studies, and an honest profit-measuring scorecard. It later learned to find real price levels in stored history and group them into strength-graded zones for a second trading approach — an honest test found no proof yet that it beat the original — but none of this deeper research was visible in the app itself until a new "Structure" tab opened it up.

That tab first drew a stock's key price levels and zones on a chart, catching and fixing one early case where the chart could go silently blank instead of honestly saying there was nothing to show. It then gained a Strategy Registry showing both trading approaches side by side, with a "Champion" badge confirming the original still holds the title — and the earlier chart bug was independently re-checked and confirmed fixed for good.

This latest chapter built the piece promised all along: a head-to-head comparison, right on the same screen. Pick a recorded dataset, click one button, and watch the app run both strategies over it, then compare trade counts, returns, win rates, and a grade-by-grade breakdown side by side — with an honest "not enough data yet" label wherever a grade hasn't traded enough to judge, and a standing reminder that every dollar figure is simulated, not real money. Hands-on testing confirmed it works correctly, including the honest result that the newer strategy still finds no trades on the sample data at hand. But the separate, independent check meant to confirm this before it's called finished couldn't reach the app when it ran this round, so the team is holding this last screen back for one more confirmation pass before the whole Structure tab — and the project's current goal — is considered complete.

What it can do today

The product lets users type in a stock ticker to watch live trade-by-trade tape reading, write trading ideas into a journal, run replay studies, and check an honest profit-and-loss scorecard. On its Structure tab, picking a stock and a point in time draws key price levels and strength-graded zones on a chart, and shows both trading approaches side by side along with which one currently holds the champion title.
