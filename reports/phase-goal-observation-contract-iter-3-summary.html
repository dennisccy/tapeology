In plain words

What you can do now

You can watch live simulated or historical tape data on the Cockpit page, browse market structure on the Structure page, and review desk screens on the Desk page. These all work the same as before.

What changed this time

Nothing changed on any screen this round. Behind the scenes, the system that watches live tape data now keeps an honest record of where each watch's data comes from and which watching session it belongs to. It also fixes a bug where a freshly restarted watch could briefly show a leftover reading from the watch it replaced.

What's next

Next, the team will prove that live watching and replaying the same recorded data give the same honest reading.
